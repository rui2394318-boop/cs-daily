**6 个最容易用错的词：**

1. **tooling** — "We need to open a new tooling for this corner." ❌ 中国人常说 "open a new mould"，但老板说的是 **tooling**（整套工装），且 tooling 不可数，别说 "a toolings"。
2. **cure** — "The part is not cured enough." ✅ 但中国人常把 cure 和 dry 混用：橡胶是 **cure**（硫化），胶水/涂层才是 **dry/cure**，说 "the rubber is dry" 老板会以为你在说表面干不干。
3. **tolerance** — "The tolerance is too small." ❌ 地道说法是 **tight tolerance**（公差紧），"small tolerance" 听起来像尺寸小。正确："The tolerance on the B-pillar is very tight."
4. **flash** — "There is some extra material." ❌ 太笼统。直接说 **flash**："We have flash on the corner joint, about 0.5 mm."
5. **pushback** — "My boss gave me a pushback." ❌ pushback 不可数，且搭配是 **get pushback from sb / push back on sth**："I got some pushback from the customer on the timing."
6. **align on** — "We need to align the schedule." ⚠️ align 后面接人/意见时要用 **align on / align with**："Let's align on the tooling date before we confirm."

## Part 3 连词与连接词 6 个（4 分钟）

| 连词 | 作用 | 例句 | 中国人常犯的错 |
|---|---|---|---|
| however | 转折（书面/正式口语） | The extrusion looks fine. However, the corner joint still shows flash. | 放在句尾说 "...however."，口语里应放句首或句中，且后面加逗号。 |
| whereas | 对比两件事 | The flocking line is stable, whereas the coating line still has defects. | 和 while 混用无妨，但 whereas 只能对比，不能表时间。 |
| therefore | 因果（结论） | The cure time was too short; therefore, the adhesion failed. | 口语里连用 therefore 显得生硬，一个汇报用一次就够。 |
| as long as | 条件（只要） | We can ship on Friday as long as the cure test passes. | 说成 "as long as ... then ..."，then 是多余的。 |
| unless | 否定条件（除非） | We won't change the tooling unless the customer approves. | 后面误加 not："unless we don't..." 是双重否定，错。 |
| due to | 原因（后接名词） | The delay is due to a die change on the extruder. | 说 "due to we changed the die"，due to 后面只能接名词/名词短语。 |

**三句串成一段连贯汇报：**
"The flocking trial is finished, **and** the adhesion looks good. **However**, the corner joint still has flash. **Therefore**, we'd like to open the tooling slightly — **as long as** you're OK with the tolerance change."

## Part 4 法国口音听力训练（8 分钟，重点）

### 4.1 法国人说英语的发音特征

| 特征 | 他会说成 | 你要听成 | 例子 |
|---|---|---|---|
| h 音脱落/乱加 | 'ave a problem / h-email | have a problem / email | "We 'ave a problem with the 'ardness." |
| th → s / z | sree, zis, wiz | three, this, with | "I sink zis is OK." = "I think this is OK." |
| 词尾重音后移 | deveLOpment, paRAmeter | deVELopment, paRAMeter | "The paraMEEter is out of tolerance." |
| /r/ 小舌音 | 喉音 r（像漱口） | 正常 r | "The rubbeR paRt" 听起来像 "heubbeh" |
| /ɪ/ 与 /iː/ 不分 | sheet / shit 同音 | 靠上下文判断 | "Check the sheet" 可能是 "check the seat" |
| -ing 读成 -ingg | mouldingg, coatingg | moulding, coating | 结尾多一个硬 g，别当成新词 |
| v / w 混淆 | wery, veek | very, week | "It's wery important" = "very important" |
| 词尾辅音吞掉 | projec, tes | project, test | "the projec is late" |
| 数字读法 | fifteen / fifty 分不清 | 靠重音：fifTEEN vs FIFty | "in fifteen days" 常被听成 "fifty days" |

### 4.2 三个高频句的「他说的 → 你听的」对照

**句 1：**
- 原句：*I think we have a problem with the hardness on the B-pillar.*
- 他大概会说：*"I sink we 'ave a problem wiz ze 'ardness on ze B-pillar."*
- 你脑子里还原：sink→think，'ave→have，wiz→with，ze→the，'ardness→hardness。关键词是 **hardness**（硬度）和 **B-pillar**，听到 "problem + B-pillar + 'ardness" 就能锁定。

**句 2：**
- 原句：*The corner joint is still showing some flash after the die change.*
- 他大概会说：*"Ze corner joint is still showing some flash after ze die change."*（joint 尾音吞掉，flash 读得很快）
- 你还原：听到 "corner join...flash...die change"，就是接角还有飞边，跟换模头有关。

**句 3：**
- 原句：*Can you check the cure time on the flocking line before Friday?*
- 他大概会说：*"Can you check ze cure time on ze flockingg line before Friday?"*（flocking 多一个 g，Friday 重音在 Fri）
- 你还原：flockingg→flocking，cure time 是关键，问题落在「周五前」。

### 4.3 假朋友（faux amis）

| 他会用的词 | 他其实想表达 | 你要怎么理解 |
|---|---|---|
| actually | 目前、现在（actuellement） | 他说 "actually we are testing" 可能是"**目前**我们在测试"，不是"其实"。 |
| eventually | 最终可能 / 也许（éventuellement） | 他说 "we will eventually do it" 常是"**有可能**做"，不是"最终一定会做"。 |
| assist | 出席、参加（assister à） | "I assisted the meeting" = "**我参加了会议**"，不是"我协助了会议"。 |
| delay | 期限、截止（délai） | "What's the delay?" 可能是"**交期是什么时候**"，不是"为什么延误"。 |
| control | 检查、核对（contrôler） | "Please control the dimensions" = "**请核对尺寸**"，不是"控制尺寸"。 |
| formation | 培训（formation） | "I need a formation on the tooling" = "**我需要一次模具培训**"。 |
| sensible | 敏感的（sensible） | "This point is sensible" = "**这点比较敏感**"，不是"这很合理"。 |

### 4.4 一段模拟对话

**老板（B）vs 你（Y）**

B: *"Y, can we 'ave a quick update on ze corner joint?"*（have / the）
Y: Sorry, I didn't quite catch that — you mean the **corner joint**? （确认关键词）
B: *"Yes, ze corner joint. I sink zere is still some flash."*（think / there）
Y: Got it — flash on the corner joint. Let me pull up the data. （复述确认）
B: *"And 'ow is ze flockingg line? Is ze adhesion OK?"*（how / flocking）
Y: Sorry, the line was a bit broken — you're asking about **adhesion on the flocking line**, right?
B: *"Yes. And what about ze cure time? We need ze data before Friday."*
Y: Understood — cure time data **before Friday**. Can I confirm: do you need it Thursday EOD, or is Friday morning OK?
B: *"Friday morning is fine. But I 'ave some pushback from ze customer on ze tooling date."*（have / the）
Y: OK — pushback from the customer on the tooling date. Could you tell me a bit more about what they said?
B: *"Zey say ze tolerance is too tight for ze current die."*（They / the）
Y: Right — the tolerance is too tight for the current die. So, to recap: I check the cure time and adhesion, send you the data Friday morning, and we discuss the die tolerance with the customer. Is that correct?
B: *"Perfect. Sanks, Y."*（Thanks）
Y: Thanks — I'll follow up by email this afternoon.
