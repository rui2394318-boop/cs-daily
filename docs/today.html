6 个最容易用错的词：

  1. peak — 中国人常说 "the number is peak"，应该用动词或形容词："Defect rate peaked at 3.2% in week 6, then came down."
  2. range — 常错说 "in the range of 2 to 3 mm" 却漏介词：正确是 "within the range of 2 to 3 mm"，或口语直接 "between 2 and 3 mm"。
  3. correlation — 中国工程师爱说 "correlation is big"，应说 "There's a strong correlation between injection pressure and the flash on the corner."
  4. attribute to — 常错成 "attribute for" 或主语搞反："We attribute the delay to the tool modification, not to the supplier."
  5. behind schedule — 常漏掉或说成 "behind of schedule"：正确 "We're two weeks behind schedule."
  6. follow up — 常错说 "I will follow this"：应说 "I'll follow up on this with the supplier and get back to you."

Part 3 连词与连接词 6 个（4 分钟）

连词 作用 例句 中国人常犯的错
however 转折（书面/正式口语） The first batch passed; however, the second showed flash. 放句尾当 "but" 用；应放句首或分号后加逗号
whereas 对比两件事 The left side is stable, whereas the right side keeps drifting. 和 while 混用没问题，但别用来表"当……时候"
therefore 因此（因果） Pressure was too low; therefore the seal didn't fill. 口语里过度使用显生硬，日常说 so 更自然
as long as 只要（条件） We can ship Friday as long as the PPAP is signed. 常错说 "as long as that…" 后面跟完整句要检查主语
unless 除非 We won't hit the date unless we add a second shift. 中文思维说 "unless not"，英语里 unless 本身已含否定
due to 由于（后接名词） The deviation is due to a temperature drift. 常错接完整句子；due to 后面只能跟名词/名词短语

三句串成一段连贯汇报：

"The defect rate peaked at 3.2% in week 6 due to a temperature drift in the oven. However, after we adjusted the setpoint, it came back within the range. Therefore, I'd say we're back on track, as long as the next batch stays steady."

Part 4 法国口音听力训练（8 分钟，重点）

4.1 法国人说英语的发音特征

特征 他会说成 你要听成 例子
h 音脱落 / 乱加 'ave / 'ardness；有时又给元音前加 h have / hardness "We 'ave a problem" = We have a problem
th → s / z sink / zis think / this "I sink zis is OK" = I think this is OK
词尾重音后移 prob-LEM、per-FECT PROB-lem、PER-fect 重音常落在最后音节
/r/ 小舌音 喉咙里发的 r 仍是 r "problem" 听起来像 "poblem"
/ɪ/ 与 /iː/ 不分 sit / seat 都读成类似 "see" 靠上下文判断 "the sheet" vs "the shit"（真实尴尬点）
-ing 读成 -ingg meeting → meeting-g meeting 词尾加硬 g
v / w 混淆 "wery" = very very "It's wery important"
词尾辅音吞掉 "tes" = test test 靠句子补全
数字 fifteen / fifty 重音不清，易混 靠重音：fif-TEEN vs FIF-ty 让他重复或写下来

4.2 三个高频句的「他说的 → 你听的」对照

句 1：
- 原句："I think we have a problem with the hardness on the B-pillar."
- 他大概会说："I sink we 'ave a prob-LEM wiz ze 'ardness on ze B-pillar."
- 你怎么还原：sink→think，wiz→with，ze→the，'ave→have，'ardness→hardness。抓住关键词 hardness + B-pillar 就够懂。

句 2：
- 原句："The defect rate peaked at three point two percent, then it dropped."
- 他大概会说："Ze defect rate peak-ed at sree point two per-SENT, zen it drop."
- 你怎么还原：sree→three，per-SENT→percent（重音在后），zen→then，drop→dropped（词尾吞掉）。听到 peak + 数字就是峰值。

句 3：
- 原句："This is very important — we are fifteen days behind schedule."
- 他大概会说："Zis is wery im-por-TANT — we are fif-TEEN days behind she-DULE."
- 你怎么还原：wery→very，fif-TEEN 重音在后=15（不是 50），she-DULE→schedule。听到重音在后就是 -teen。

4.3 假朋友（faux amis）

他会用的词 他其实想表达 你要怎么理解
actually 目前、当前（法语 actuellement） 不是"实际上"，是"现在/目前"
eventually 可能、最终也许（法语 éventuellement） 不是"最终一定"，是"也许/有可能"
assist (at) 出席、参加（法语 assister à） 不是"协助"，是"参加某个会"
delay 期限、时限（法语 délai） 不是"延误"，是"截止时间/交期"
control 检查、核对（法语 contrôler） 不是"控制"，是"检查一下"
formation 培训（法语 formation） 不是"形成"，是"培训课程"
sensible 敏感的（法语 sensible） 不是"明智的"，是"敏感/容易受影响"

4.4 一段模拟对话

老板（法式口音）： "So, why are we late on ze PPAP?"（standard: why are we late on the PPAP）
你： Sorry, I didn't quite catch that — are you asking about the PPAP timing?
老板： "Yes. Ze tool 'as a prob-LEM, and zis is wery im-por-TANT for ze customer."（the tool has a problem, this is very important）
你： Got it. Just to make sure — you mean the tool failure is the reason we're behind, correct?
老板： "Exactly. Ze 'ardness is out of ze range, so ze parts fail."（the hardness is out of the range）
你： Understood. So the root cause is the hardness deviation, and that's why the parts are out of tolerance.
老板： "Yes. And ze defect rate peak-ed at sree point two per-SENT."（peaked at three point two percent）
你： Sorry, was that three point two, or three point five? I want to get the number right.
老板： "Sree point two."（three point two）
你： Perfect, three point two. So just to confirm — root cause is hardness, peak was 3.2%, and we need a corrective action by Friday. I'll follow up with the supplier and circle back to you tomorrow.

Part 5 输出任务（2 分钟）

任务 1（开口录音）： 把下面这段因果汇报完整念 3 遍并录音，第 3 遍不看稿：

"The defect rate peaked at 3.2% in week 6 due to a temperature drift in the oven. However, after we adjusted the setpoint, it came back within the range. Therefore, we're back on track, as long as the next batch stays steady."

检查点： 录音回放，确认① due to 后面跟的是名词短语不是句子；② however 放在句首或分号后、有停顿；③ 三个连接词都念清楚没吞音；④ 全程没有卡壳超过 2 秒。做到 3 条以上算过。

任务 2（写）： 用 3–5 句写一段你今天真实工作的因果说明（延期或超差皆可），必须包含：1 个数据词（peak / dip / range 等）+ 1 个因果连接词（due to / therefore / stem from）+ 1 个假朋友词的"正确理解"（比如你想说"目前"时用 currently 而不是 actually）。

检查点： 写完后自查——① 是否有一句明确说"原因"、一句明确说"结果"；② 连接词用得对不对（due to 后是名词）；③ 整段大声念一遍是否顺口。三条全中算完成。
