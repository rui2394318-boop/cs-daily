6 个最容易用错的词

  1. decline — 中国人爱说 "The number is declining down."（重复）。
    ✅ The leak rate declined from 12 to 8 ml/min over three weeks.
    ❌ 错在：decline 本身已含"向下"，不能加 down；且它是不及物，不能说 "decline the number"。

  2. correlate — 常错说 "A is correlated to B"。
    ✅ The warpage correlates strongly with the mold temperature.
    ❌ 固定搭配是 correlate with，不是 to；表因果别用 correlate，它只讲"同涨同落"。

  3. range — 常错 "in the range from 5 to 10"。
    ✅ The hardness falls within the range of 55 to 60 Shore A.
    ❌ range 前用 within，不用 in；"from…to" 与 "range" 二选一，别叠用。

  4. account for — 常错把它只当"占比"。
    ✅ The 2-day delay is accounted for by the late tooling delivery.（解释原因）
    ✅ EPDM accounts for 60% of the cost.（占比）
    ❌ 两个意思都要会，会上老板问 "How do you account for this?"＝"你怎么解释这个？"

  5. offset — 常错 "offset up the cost"。
    ✅ The lower material price offsets the extra freight.
    ❌ offset 是及物动词，直接接宾语，不加 up/off。

  6. threshold — 发音常错成 /ˈθreshould/。
    ✅ Once the compression set exceeds the threshold, we stop the line.
    ❌ th 要咬舌 /θ/，结尾 -old 读 /hoʊld/，不是 "hold" 分开念。

Part 3 连词与连接词 6 个（4 分钟）

连词 作用 例句 中国人常犯的错
however 转折（书面/正式口语） The first batch passed; however, the second showed warpage. 后面必须加逗号，且不能和 but 连用（"but however" 是错的）
whereas 对比两件事 The left side is fine, whereas the right side shows flash. 只用于对比，不用于转折让步；后面接完整句子
therefore 因此（因果，正式） The mold temp was unstable; therefore, we re-ran the samples. 是副词不是连词，两个句子要用分号或句号隔开，不能只用逗号
as long as 只要（条件） We can ship as long as QC signs off today. 后面接从句（主谓齐全），别接名词："as long as the approval" ❌
unless 除非（否定条件） We won't restart unless the temp is stable. 本身含否定，别再说 "unless not"
due to 由于（后接名词） The delay is due to the late tooling. due to 接名词，because of 也一样；表原因的原因状语别用 "due to the mold is hot" ❌

3 句串成一段连贯汇报：

The leak rate peaked at 12 ml/min in week 2, whereas it has dropped to 6 since we changed the compound. Therefore, we believe the root cause is the filler content. However, we still need two more batches to confirm the trend before we close the issue.

Part 4 法国口音听力训练（8 分钟，重点）

4.1 法国人说英语的发音特征

特征 他会说成 你要听成 例子
h 音脱落 / 乱加 "I 'ave a problem" / "he is 'appy" 开头的 h 常消失，元音开头的词可能多出 h have→'ave；hour→'our；happy→'appy
th 变 s/z "sink" / "zis" th→/s/ 或 /z/ think→sink；this→zis；three→sree
词尾重音 "deVELopment" 重音后移 重音常落在词尾 development→develoPMENT；analysis→anaLYsis
/r/ 小舌音 喉音 r，像"喝" r 发得靠后、带摩擦 report→"heport"；problem→"p-roblem"
/ɪ/ 与 /iː/ 不分 "sheet" 与 "shit" 同音 短 i 拉长 sit→seat；fill→feel；chip→cheap
-ing 读成 -ingg "meetingg" 词尾 g 硬加出来 testing→testingg；reading→readingg
v / w 混淆 "wery" / "vest" v 和 w 互换 very→wery；west→vest；invite→inwite
词尾辅音清化/脱落 "collec" / "produc" 结尾 t/d/k 常吞掉 product→produc；collect→collec
数字读法 fifteen / fifty 混 重音位置不同：FIF-teen vs FIF-ty fifteen 重音在后，fifty 重音在前

4.2 三个高频句的「他说的 → 你听的」对照

① 原句： I think we have a problem with the hardness on the B-pillar.
他大概会说： "I sink we 'ave a p-roblem wiz ze 'ardness on ze B-pillar."
你怎么还原： sink→think（th→s）；'ave→have（h 脱落）；wiz→with；ze→the（th→z）；'ardness→hardness。听到 "p-roblem" 的喉音 r 别慌，就是 problem。

② 原句： The leak rate is still fluctuating between eight and twelve.
他大概会说： "Ze leak r-rate is still fluktuating between eight and twelve."
你怎么还原： ze→the；r-rate 的喉音 r；fluctuating 重音后移成 flukTUating；eight/twelve 数字要抓准，别听成 eighteen。

③ 原句： We need to push back the deadline by fifteen days.
他大概会说： "We need to push back ze deadline by fifTEEN days."
你怎么还原： fifTEEN 重音在后＝15；如果是 FIFty 重音在前＝50。这是最容易出大事的地方，听到数字一定要复述确认。

4.3 假朋友（faux amis）

他会用的词 他其实想表达 你要怎么理解
actually 法语 actuellement＝"目前、现在" 他说 actually 常是"currently / at the moment"，不是"实际上"
eventually 法语 éventuellement＝"可能、或许" 他说 eventually 常是"possibly / maybe"，不是"最终"
assist (at) 法语 assister à＝"参加、出席" "I assisted the meeting"＝"我参加了会议"，不是"我协助了"
delay 法语 délai＝"期限、时限" "What's the delay?" 可能是问"截止期是什么时候？"
control 法语 contrôle＝"检查、检验" "quality control" 他可能指"质量检验/抽检"
formation 法语 formation＝"培训" "a formation on the new press"＝"新压机的培训"
sensible 法语 sensible＝"敏感的" 他说 sensible 可能是"敏感"，不是"明智的"

4.4 一段模拟对话

老板（Boss）： "So, I sink (I think) ze (the) p-roblem (problem) is wiz (with) ze 'ardness (hardness) on ze B-pillar. How do you ac-count for zat?"
你： "Sorry, just to make sure I've got it — you're asking why the hardness on the B-pillar is out of spec, right?"
Boss： "Yes. Ze value is fluctuating between fifty-five and sixty-five. Zat's (that's) too wide."
你： "Understood. It stems from the mold temperature — it peaked at 190 last week, whereas it should stay within the range of 175 to 180."
Boss： "And ze root cause?"
你： "The root cause is a faulty thermocouple. We attribute the drift to that sensor. Therefore we've replaced it and re-ran two batches."
Boss： "Good. But we cannot push back the deadline by fifTEEN (fifteen) days."
你： "Sorry — fifteen or fifty? I want to be sure."
Boss： "FifTEEN. Fifteen days maximum."
你： "Got it, fifteen. As long as QC signs off by Friday, we stay on track. I'll follow up on the 12th and send the trend chart."

Part 5 输出任务（2 分钟）

任务 1（开口录音，3 分钟）
对着手机录下面这段，模拟向老板汇报"为什么延期"：

The PPAP slipped by ten days. It stems from a tool failure on the B-pillar mold. The leak rate peaked at 12 ml/min, whereas the target is below 8. Therefore we re-ran two batches, and it's now stabilizing at 7. However, we still need one more batch to confirm the trend. As long as QC signs off by Friday, we stay on track.

检查点：
- ☐ 每个 th（the / think / stems）都咬舌，没读成 s/z；
- ☐ fifteen / fifty 类数字若出现，重音位置对；
- ☐ 四个连词（whereas / therefore / however / as long as）都读出来且停顿自然；
- ☐ 整段 45 秒内说完，不卡壳。

任务 2（写，5 分钟）
用 3–5 句写一段"超差原因+结果"，必须包含：root cause、correlate with、therefore、offset。

范例结构（自己改写，别照抄）：

The hardness on the B-pillar is out of spec. The root cause is the mold temperature, which correlates strongly with the cooling time. Therefore we shortened the cycle by 4 seconds. The lower scrap rate offsets the extra cost of the new sensor.

检查点：
- ☐ 4 个目标词/短语都用上且搭配正确（correlate with，不是 to）；
- ☐ 因果链清楚：原因→措施→结果，读者一遍能懂；
- ☐ 没有 "because… so…" 这种中式双因果。
