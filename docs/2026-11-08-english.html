6 个最容易用错的词

  1. profile — 中国人常说 "the shape of the seal"，其实行业里就说 profile。We changed the profile of the glass run channel to reduce the rattle. 错误：把 profile 理解成"简介/档案"。
  2. gap and flushness — 常说 "the gap is big"，要成对说。Please check gap and flushness at the belt line before we sign off. 错误：只说 gap，漏掉 flushness（段差）。
  3. trade-off — 常被说成 "choose which one is better"，应说 trade-off。There's a trade-off between CLD and door closing effort. 错误：说成 "trade"（贸易）。
  4. baseline — 常说 "the old version"，正式点用 baseline。Option A is our baseline; option B is 8% cheaper. 错误：说成 "base line" 两个词分开念。
  5. criterion / criteria — 单复数常混。Cost is the main criterion; durability and cost are the two criteria. 错误：说 "one criteria"。
  6. lean toward — 想说"我倾向"常说 "I'm tend to"，正确是 lean toward 或 I tend to。I lean toward B, but I want to see the CLD data first. 错误：漏介词，说成 lean B。

Part 3 连词与连接词 6 个（4 分钟）

连词 作用 例句 中国人常犯的错
however 转折（书面/正式口语） Option A is cheaper. However, the CLD margin is too small. 句首用 however 后仍加 but：~~However, but...~~
whereas 对比两件事 A is cheaper, whereas B gives us more safety margin. 和 while 混用后接句子缺主语；whereas 后必须完整从句
therefore 因果（结论） The flocking is worn; therefore we need a new run channel. 口语里滥用 therefore，显得生硬；日常多说 so
as long as 条件（只要） We can go with B as long as the cost stays under target. 后面接 that 从句时误加 will：~~as long as it will...~~
unless 否定条件（除非） We won't change the profile unless wind noise fails. 和 if not 重复：~~unless if...~~
in terms of 就……而言（限定维度） In terms of cost, A is better; in terms of durability, B is. 后面接动词原形：~~in terms of reduce cost~~（应 reducing）

三句串成一段汇报：

Option A is cheaper whereas option B gives us a bigger CLD margin. However, the flocking on B wears faster in our durability test. Therefore, I lean toward B as long as we can fix the flocking before SOP.

Part 4 法国口音听力训练（8 分钟，重点）

4.1 法国人说英语的发音特征

特征 他会说成 你要听成 例子
h 音脱落 / 乱加 'ardness、'ave；又在元音前加 h hardness、have "the 'ardness on the B-pillar" = hardness
th → s / z sink、zis、wiz think、this、with "I sink we should..." = I think
词尾重音后移 comFORTable、perFORmance COMfortable、perFORmance（英语重音） "the perFORmance is OK"
/r/ 小舌音 喉音 r，几乎听不清 仍是 r "roof drip" 听起来含糊
/ɪ/ 与 /iː/ 不分 sit / seat 同音 靠上下文判断 "a seat problem" 可能是 sit
-ing 读成 -ingg meeting-g、nothing-g meeting、nothing "the meeting-g is at two"
v / w 混淆 wery、vest very、west "it's wery tight" = very
词尾辅音吞掉 produc'、cos' product、cost "the cos' is too high" = cost
数字 fifteen / fifty 重音位置不同，易混 fifteen=重音在后；fifty=重音在前 "fifTEEN percent" ≠ fifty

4.2 三个高频句的「他说的 → 你听的」

① 原句： "I think we have a problem with the hardness on the B-pillar seal."
- 他大概说："I sink we 'ave a problem wiz ze 'ardness on ze B-pillar seal."
- 脑内还原：sink→think；'ave→have；wiz→with；ze→the；'ardness→hardness。关键词：hardness（硬度）、B-pillar seal（B 柱密封条）。

② 原句： "Can you compare the cost of the glass run channel and the belt line weatherstrip?"
- 他大概说："Can you compare ze cos' of ze glass run channel and ze belt line wezerstrip?"
- 脑内还原：cos'→cost；wez-er-strip→weatherstrip（th→z）；run channel 的 r 含糊但词本身清楚。抓两个名词就懂在问对比。

③ 原句： "The roof drip molding is fifteen millimeters too low — that's a showstopper."
- 他大概说："Ze roof drip molding is fifTEEN millimeters too low — zat's a showstopper."
- 脑内还原：fifTEEN 重音在后=15（不是 50）；zat's→that's；showstopper 是关键词，听到就知是严重问题。

4.3 假朋友（faux amis）

他会用的词 他其实想表达 你要怎么理解
actually 法语 actuellement = 目前、当前 听到 actually 先想"currently / at the moment"，不是"实际上"
eventually 法语 éventuellement = 可能、也许 理解为"possibly / maybe"，不是"最终"
assist (at) 法语 assister à = 出席、参加 "I assisted the meeting" = 我参加了会议，不是"协助"
delay 法语 délai = 期限、交期 "what's the delay?" 可能问"交期是什么"，不是"延误"
control 法语 contrôle = 检查、检验 "we need a control" = 要做一次检查，不一定是"控制"
formation 法语 formation = 培训 "a formation on sealing" = 一次密封条培训
sensible 法语 sensible = 敏感的 "this area is sensible" = 这里很敏感（不是"明智"）

4.4 一段模拟对话

老板： "So, between A and B — which one do you sink is better?"（think）
你： Sorry, just to be sure — you're asking which option I'd recommend, right?
老板： Yes. Ze main criTEria are cos' and durability.
你： Got it. A is cheaper, whereas B gives us more margin on the CLD. But B has a flocking wear issue.
老板： 'Ow bad is ze flocking? Is it a showstopper?
你： I didn't catch the first word — was that "how bad"?
老板： Yes, 'ow bad.
你： It's borderline. Not a showstopper yet, but I'd like one more durability run before we commit.
老板： OK. Zen we lean toward B, as long as ze flocking passes.
你： Exactly. I'll send the comparison by Friday and flag the flocking risk. Does that work?

Part 5 输出任务（2 分钟）

任务 1（开口录音）： 用手机录一段 40 秒的对比汇报，必须念下面这三句（可换成你手上的真实零件）：

"Option A uses the door aperture seal as baseline, whereas option B changes the glass run channel profile. However, B's belt line weatherstrip shows a gap issue. Therefore, I lean toward A as long as the roof drip molding retention passes."
- 检查点：① however / whereas / therefore / as long as 四个词都念出来且没卡壳；② 录音回放能听清每个连词；③ 全程不出现 "um... how to say"。

任务 2（写）： 写 3–5 句，用英文比较你手上真实的方案 A 和方案 B，至少用上 trade-off、baseline、criterion 各一次，并给出你的建议。
- 检查点：① 三个词都用对（criterion 单数、criteria 复数别混）；② 有一句明确写出 "I lean toward / I recommend..."；③ 有一句说清取舍点（cost vs. performance 之类）。写完自己读一遍，能一口气念顺就算过。
